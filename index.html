FAQs

Questions families ask us.

Valuation

How accurate is the online estimate?

It uses today's rate and the purity and weight you enter. Where something is unclear we assume conservatively, so the final figure after testing is usually the same or higher.

My jewellery has no hallmark. Can I still sell it?

Yes. Most pieces bought before hallmarking became mandatory carry no HUID. We test the purity by XRF in front of you and pay for the gold that's actually there.

How do you work out the price?

Net metal weight × today's rate for that purity, minus the fee for your category. Stones, lac and solder are weighed out first and listed on your report.

Selling

Do I have to sell after the valuation?

No. Valuation is free and the return is free. Many families come only for the report.

How will I be paid?

By IMPS, NEFT or RTGS to an account in your name, usually the same day you accept. We don't pay out in cash.

What documents do I need?

A PAN card and address proof for every sale, and a bank account in your name.

Can you value diamonds, polki and kundan?

Yes. Natural diamonds are graded in person. Polki and kundan are valued for their gold, and we tell you when a piece is worth more kept whole.

Tax & paperwork

Is there tax on selling old gold?

Selling jewellery you own may attract capital gains tax depending on how long you've held it. For gold bought before 2001, a valuation as on 1 April 2001 can be used as the cost. Check your case with your CA.

Do you give an invoice?

Yes, every sale and exchange comes with an invoice and the test report for your records.
